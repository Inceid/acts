# Map-of-Essay
In its [culminative form](<Culminative Forms>), a dynamic essay will have a map-of-essay that visually depicts its conceptual progression. This functions as a visual “executive summary” for the reader. 

# Preamble 
Essays are prolonged semi-organized excursions into a particular subject put into writing. For someone like me whose thoughts flit rapidly between different topics *but are able to cycle back to the same topic reliably*, the essay is a fairly restrictive exercise. My knowledge and take on a particular subject may evolve radically over time, but the essay can only represent my thoughts at the time of writing. So the question is: how do we come up with an essay-like format for an intellectual excursion *that reflects the development of its thinker’s views* on the topic over time? And how do we do so by preserving the overall integrity of the essay’s original structure? My answer is the dynamic essay format, described roughly below. 

# Definition 
A **dynamic essay** is an essay that evolves over time. A **versioned** dynamic essay is a dynamic essay with discrete snapshots to capture its distinct forms. 

There’s nothing that special about dynamic essays except that they show multiple versions of an essay’s representation over time in a manner convenient for a reader. 

# Analog: Software
Versioning a dynamic essay is analogous to versioning software.
