# Definition
**Investigationalism** is a general approach to knowledge and learning that renders each fact, theory, or idea a subject of investigation. 

Investigation is the process of questioning, inquiring, manipulating, synthesizing, and exchanging information in search of a common understanding or notion of truth regarding that information. 

# Motivation
For knowledge work that’s turned into autopiloting, investigationalism is the most reliable protective activity against escapist tendencies. 

This is particularly useful in moments of frustration or exhaustion when studying lots of complicated information. A pessimistic response to such moments is “why do I really have to do all this? What is all this for? Why can’t I just go home or give up?”. An investigationalist response says, “there must be something I’m missing. Let me step back and simply observe.”

Investigationalism enables observation of self-as-system. Observation enables one to infer rules and patterns about one’s behavior. And it’s through an understanding of those rules and patterns that one can begin to intervene to change one’s circumstances and mindset for the better.
