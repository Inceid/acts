# Definition
A **variational style** is a learning and creative strategy whereby several active variables within an environment are altered at the same time. 

This deliberately induce a cognitive challenge in the learner/creater to adapt to the environment and think on their feet. 

Applied in the setting of [[Current Active Cycle]], a variational style involves substitution of elements within an active cycle to maintain cognitive engagement, as well as varying the settings wherein the work is done.

# Motivation 
The advantage of a variational style is that it promotes active, dynamic thinking on the part of the learner/creator to adapt to ever-changing environments. 

Drawbacks include sustained stress on the learner/creator as well as a lack of clarity as to which substituted variable improves performance if multiple variables are being substituted at the same time.
