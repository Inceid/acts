# Definition
**Decognitivization** is the reversal of cognitivization. Given a cognitivized set of thought for which there exists an incentive to “unwrap” or reassemble the thought back to its original form, decognitivization consists of a set of operations to reverse cognitive encodings of an object. 

# Motivation
A vividly imaginative mind can become [intoxicated](<Vision Intoxication.md>) on its own dreams. The reason for this is natural: many dreams can’t be accomplished at the present time (at least not as easily) as we go through life.

The mind has to eventually decide: which is better? The dream dreamed, or the dream realized? And if we decide that the dream realized is unambiguously better, how are we to realize a dream that’s lodged itself deep into our thought and prefers to stay there, rather than venture into a hostile world where it will inevitably encounter criticism and rejection? 

In this context, the purpose of decognitivization is to pull the dream out of the mind by specifically casting it into a concrete physical form.

In other contexts, decognivitization may be an excellent method for methodically navigating and testing very abstract systems. Consider the abacus, a physical calculator, which decognitivizes arithmetic into a game-like machine. 

During my gap years in Maryland, when I was tutoring young kids (5-6) at Mathnasium, I would often pull out an abacus for a student who was struggling with arithmetic for two-digit numbers. Once I explained the rules of moving the chips from left to right when adding and right to left when subtracting, as well as the rules for adding tens, addition and subtraction suddenly became very intuitive for them. And they liked using it!

*Fig 1*: An Abacus.

The abacus is by no means strictly necessary for performing arithmetic. But it effectively decognitivizes arithmetic operations by implementing them in a structure that obeys decimal rules for arithmetic. By mastering this physical structure, one effectively clarifies and implements the “dream” of arithmetic, and opens doors to higher levels of mathematics. 

The claim here is that there are many other kinds of decognitivization for dreams and ideas more complex than arithmetic.
