# Definition 
**Emulation** is the process of copying and representing an object in a modality different from the original object. 

An **emulon** is a persistent kernel, mental system, or explicit instantiation/virtualization of a system that seeks to accurately emulate that system or a memory thereof from experience. 

# Implementation 
Emulation is a natural consequence of repeated interaction with any system that modifies behavior. 

The more you interact with visual reel-based social media, the more your thoughts become like reels. The more you interact with generative AI, the more generative and autocompletionist your thoughts become. The more you interact with music and music platforms, the more your mind becomes a neverending soundtrack.
