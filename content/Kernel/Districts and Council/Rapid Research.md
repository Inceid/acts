# Definition 
**Rapid Research** is any technique for learning and rapidly deploying specialized knowledge in a particular scenario. It’s also a handy technique for rendering “rabbit holes” and “deep dives” an active process with a concrete deliverable at the end of the dive.

# Motivation
If I want to get anything from [[metapcms]] done, then I have to realize that *this is a shit ton of work*, and *I don’t have a lot of time*. So I’ll have to work rapidly to 1) shore up my fundamentals, 2) implement projects extremely quickly, 3) rapidly refine my cross-disciplinary and intra-disciplinary understanding of each field as I move forward, and 4) learn the medicine as well as I can, since I can’t do any of this if I don’t do well in medical school.
