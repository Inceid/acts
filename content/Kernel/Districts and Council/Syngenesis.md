# Definition
**Syngenesis** is the act of simultaneous creation and consumption. 

# Usage
Syngenesis refers to consumption and creation that occurs simultaneously or with minimal separation in space and time. The moment you read a book and have an idea to write a commentary on it, you start one. The moment you watch a video and have an idea to respond, you write a response. 

Syngenesis is especially helpful for my learning, as whatever I am reading, I refactor it into my own format, language, or other personalized form. I make it personal by recreating it, hence learning as syngenesis.

Syngenesis follows an “eager” read-execute model of production, similarly to how many computers read and execute code: they read instructions and execute them as soon as they are read. In informal terms, it’s a method of immediately applying principles that I learn in a form that admits of clear, rapid iterative feedback and improvement. 

# Implementation 
## Reading 
Writing while reading effectively implements syngenesis: it couples consumption with active interpretation and creation. 

Reflection, interrogation, and mental modeling while reading also implements syngenesis but leaves less of a persistent state after the reading is complete.
