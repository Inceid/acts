# Preamble
This essay describes rules for applying [`descend`](<Op descend>) to a question, or when to descend into an inquiry, project, or rabiit hole on a question of interest. 

# The `RI` Filter 
You may [descend](<Op descend>) when a candidate descent meets one of the following criteria: 

1. Candidate admits of a reflection from `RI`: some component of `RI` is capable of reflecting or emulating it.
2. Progress in the candidate descent leads to progress or refinement of some part of `RI` *directly*.
3. Candidate can be emulated/encoded within `RI` as an additional element. 
4. Candidate advances my ability to directly apply part of `RI` to achieve socially a valuable outcome. 

All projects must thus pass an "`RI` filter".
