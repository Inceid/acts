# Definition 
**Practical Foundationalism** (PF) is an approach to learning that interleaves theoretical development with immediate modularized implementation and self-directed projects.

PF is distinguished from a classical approach to foundationalist learning, which prioritizes learning a full body of foundational knowledge before applying it in practice. 

PF is also distinguished from purely applied (non-foundational) modalities of learning, which emphasize building and applying whatever a learner knows and correcting or further investigating *only* what the learner is interested in. It eschews theory or standardization.
