# Definition
**Dialectasis** or **Dialectical anticipation** is the process of anticipating possible or probably future events and recording your rough responses to them ahead of time.

**inspo:** [pathoinversion](<Pathoinversion>) of paranoia and anxious anticipation of the future (GAD)

Dialectasis is a psychological analog of a [[Differential Diagnosis]].

# Realization  
In some cases, certain branches of dialants may actually occur. In this case we say a given branch of a dialant has “realized” on a particular date.
