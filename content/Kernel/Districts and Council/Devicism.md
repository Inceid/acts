# Definition
**Devicism** is based on the idea that skill with using devices, rule-based generative/interpretive/expressive systems or tools for personal use, are imperative for achieving any complex goal or task. 

A **device** is any idea, technique, mechanism, object meant to fulfill a specific purpose. 

A good device is one that fulfills its purpose faster or with less effort than if a human did not use it. 

A good device is one that enables means to fulfill its purpose that would not exist without the device. 

Excellent devices compress, amplify, and refine the expressive energy of their authors.

# Examples.
>Language is a device for communication. 
>Religion is a device. “Useful fictions” are devices. 

>Gunas and Pujas are cognitive devices - they structure, process, and render thought in an effable and reinforcable form. 

>An arm is a device. It has an object with certain rules, use cases, and limitations that can be used to fulfill a particular set of purposes. 

>The body is a device.
