>All of us derive a substantial fraction of our thought from salient experiences. Might as well render them explicit. 

# Definition 
An **infusion** is a deliberate injection of sensory/experiential data.
*Examples:*
- Shadowing a physician serves as an infusion of experience to motivate further engagement with medical knowledge. 
- Seeing patients in clinic serves as an agentic infusion to motivate further pursuit of being a clinician.
- Meeting up with a mentor-figure or friend from the past and catching up on life. 
It's good to schedule infusions as part of a routine or cycle. 

## The Right "Dosage" for an Infusion
Infusions should be scheduled to be regular, but the frequency depends on a few factors. First, all infusions should advance inquiries I already have. For a review of my priority inquiries, see [[Kernel/अन्वेषण]]. 

In an ideal scenario, infusions are scheduled so that we can complete the maximum amount of work on our inquiries using the energy of the infusion before the next one. For example, let's say I meet with a mentor every 6 months. If this dosage is ideal, that means I can complete at most 6 months worth of mentor-advice-augmented work before I need the same infusion again.

It's important to recognize that no two infusions are necessarily equivalent: meeting with Dean Archer may not provide the same type of energy as meeting with Dean Boucher.
