`dual` [[Vision Intoxication]]
>Dreaming is fun. Implementing dreams, however, is grimy, agonizing, soulcrushing.

# Definition
**Oneiromancy** or **Visioncasting**/**Dreamcasting** is the art of taking a written vision and casting it into the world in some implementation. 

# Implementation
Dreams are just vivid ideas; I describe how I implement ideas [here](<Ideation and Execution>).

# Pitfalls 
## Expectation-process mismatch
The experience of oneiromancy may be very different from the experience of the dream itself. Oneiromancy may be unpleasant, boring, tedious. 

The way to deal with this is to recognize it clearly for what it is, then to re-evaluate whether the dream is worth the effort to cast - whether the dream realized is worth the risk of failure and cost incurred to attempt to cast it.
