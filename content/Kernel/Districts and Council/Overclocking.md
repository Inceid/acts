# Definition
**Sensory overclocking** is the process by which we overwhelm our sensory reception of a stimulating phenomenon for the purpose of gaining control over that phenomenon.

The premise of sensory overclocking is that it desensitizes your capacities of sensibility against further stimulation. As our bodies adapt to a stimulus neurologically by increasing the dopaminergic/x-ergic excitation thresholds for that stimulus, overclocking that stimulus prevents us from locking ourselves into a nonterminating excitation sequence whereby we continue to elicit the same stimulus and shirk our responsibilities.
