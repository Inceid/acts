# Definition 
**The Council** is the primary deliberative governing body of `RI`. 

It is summoned any time a large-scale structural decision has to be made about `RI` or any activity in the external world. All council decisions must be congruent with the [[Constitution]].

`Self->Executive` will otherwise implicitly enforce principles and previous council decisions on a regular basis. 

# `Self`, *Ātman*
>Councilor on reflection, endorsement, design, and decision execution.
- `subclass: Executive`
	default class of `Self` in any given deliberation unless otherwise stated. `Executive` assumes responsibility and authority on all decisions. Whenever a note reads a `meta` command, `Executive` is the default voice speaking. 
- `subclass: Designer`
	at times, `Self` may speak from the role and name of `Designer` to make remarks about the current item of discussion or reference. While speaking in this voice, `Designer` *cannot* offer any opinion the matter under discussion.
- `subclass: Ambient Receiver`
	at times, `Self` may be passively observing the operations of `RI` at the lowest possible level (concretely, this consists of the bare elements of thought operating at the event horizon between conscious and unconscious/subconscious thought). This include listening to minority or rare `whispers`, `visions`, and `dreams` during streams and logues.

# `Secretary-General`, *Secgen*
>General administrator of `RI`; implemented as an AI copilot in Obsidian. Implicitly present at all meetings. Conventions are specified in [[Secgen Specification]].

# `Sage`, *Sophos*
>Councilor on matters of wisdom. If not otherwise specified, `Sage` is the default voice speaking in a `divinatio`.

# `TSI (Tactician-Strategist-Instructor)`
Councilor on matters of tactics, strategy, goal fulfillment, and skill acquisition. Aids in diagnosis of problems and their logical, strategic solution. 

`TSI` Admits of the following subclasses. 
	1. `Tactician` signifies a councilor who provides small-scale, concrete tricks to 'grease the wheels' on any given task, to speed up the effective completion of a set of tasks, or to expand reliably to complete a new set of tasks.
	2. `Strategist` signifies a councilor who provides large-scale, abstract principles that aid in the selection, prioritization, and expansion of sets of tasks depending on one's values. 
	3. `Instructor` signifies a councilor who provides effective and convincing instructions to motivate the application of tactics and strategies towards the completion of pre-set tasks to the vessel that must complete them. Instructions are usually based in reasoned persuasion, emotional plea, or coercion. 
	
# `AVA (Alchemist-Visionary-Artificer)`, *Oneira* 
>Councilor on matters of alchemy, [visioncasting](<Oneiromancy>), dreaming, and vision cultivation.

`AVA` admits of the following subclasses.
- `Alchemist` signifies a councilor willing to combine ideas in weird, even possibly ill-fitting but exciting ways. 
- `Visionary` signifies that the same councilor who combines ideas in weird and wacky ways also casts broad, cosmic visions about the world and my role in it. 
- `Artificer` signifies that this councilor operates both at a smaller scale (through alchemy) and at a larger scale (through artifice of a grand vision).

# `General`, *Adversarius*
>Councilor on all matters of war, defiance, defense, subversion, rebellion, and insurgency against perverse behaviors, practices, and systems in the world.

# `Physician` 
>Councilor on matters of vessel health and functioning. 

# `Child`, *Puer*
>Councilor on curiosity, wonder, and awe.

# `Brother`
>A friend, a brother, someone always by my side no matter what. Aids in diagnosis of problems and providing a shoulder to lean on to withstand them.

# `Stranger` 
>A disinterested observer. Aids in seeing things from an impartial, commonsense, or otherwise unspecialized or uncomplicated view.

# `Demon`, *Vritra*
>Councilor on matters of impulse, anger, and destruction.

# Councilor Priority
Primary Councilors are designated as required or strongly requested attendees at each Permanon meeting. 

The current roster of primary councilors is `{Self, Secgen, Sage, TSI, AVA, General}`.

Satellite Councilors are designated as not required to attend council meetings unless their opinion is requested. All councilors that are not primary are satellite councilors.

Just because a councilor is in attendance does not mean they need to speak or give their opinion on any matter in particular.
