# Definition 
An **ambient kernel** or **mobile kernel** is a simple instantiation of a kernel when `RI` or the full structure of the kernel is otherwise inaccessible. 

# Elements 
Any mobile kernel will consist of three streams to organize thought: 
1. Primary $\mathbb{1}$ or $|$
2. Parallel $\mathbb{P}$ or $||$ 
3. Meta or $\top$ 

The primary stream $\mathbb{1}$ is a strict sequence of thought related to a topic that the conscious mind is thinking about and that corresponds to one’s circumstances. 

The parallel stream $\mathbb{P}$ houses all thoughts occuring outside of the primary stream or circumstances, fleeting or otherwise. 

The meta stream $\top$ comprises a persistent metacognitive narration of the events in the primary and parallel streams. 

# History 
I designed my first mobile kernel during the CBSE in December 2024. It was my first NBME exam; taking the exam made me extremely frustrated. I couldn’t focus on the questions, so I decided to think about how to recover a kernel structure while away from my computer and use it to help me organize my thought generally. 

I skipped a bunch of the questions and instead spent time writing out elements of a mobile kernel and simulating some experiences in my head using the kernel.
