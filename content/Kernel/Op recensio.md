# Notation
`recensio` \<admonitio message\>

`recn` \<message \>

`Recensio`
(`Speaker:` \<member of council speaking\>)

# Definition
A **Recensio** is a reappraisal of a past event from the present or future.

If the speaker is from the future, the argument is either a version of oneself or a councilmember.
