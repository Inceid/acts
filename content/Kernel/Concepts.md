# Primordia 
`gen` [[Thought Capture]], [[Congruence]], [[Staging]] → what kinds of notes are these? What is their general form? Rather, what’s a suitable general form to get things off the ground for notes like these?

# Preamble 
It’ll benefit us to have a fully generalized format for spawning different fundamental kinds of notes. One of the most fundamental kinds of notes is a *concept note*, which, to ensure maximal flexibility of application of this note form, we say refers to any note that instantiates a novel or personal concept. 

# Definition 
A **concept** is a general idea; in the setting of `RI`, a concept is a novel general idea.

A **concept note** is a type of note that defines and implements a novel general idea. 

# Implementation 
There is no strict prerequisite for a thought to become a concept other than that it conform to a minimal concept note. That is to say, it must admit of a definition and implementation (a specific form of the concept usable in daily life).

The structure of a concept note is as previously outlined:
1. (optional) *Primordia*, which describes motivations and any fleeting thoughts behind the inception of the concept informally; 
2. (optional) *Preamble*, a formal introduction to the motivation behind the concept; 
3. Definition, a definition of the concept in clear, plain language; 
				4. Implementation, an implementation or specific example or usable version of the concept in practice.
