# Definition
A **Divinatio** is a message from your future self to your present self, or a vision into the future. 

Divinatios consist of a speaker or source and lifespan. The speaker issues the advice requested. The lifespan is the frequency of the divinatio. 

# Body
Suraj,

This is a message from the future. I am your future; you are my present. 

I suspect you will go through many agonies and pleasures in the next few years. Many high highs and low lows. In moments of despair and doubt, it is critically important to maintain an open channel to the voices that have guided you in recent years. To your council, to your future self, to your past child self.
`Sage`

# Divinatio types 
>A `divinatio` admits of multiple types distinguished according to its element types (speaker, )
## 

### Council `divinatio` 
A council `divinatio` calls a council member to give advice on a situation. 

### Adapter `divinatio`
An adapter `divinatio` calls a version of yourself that is well-adjusted to a life of phenomena-based activity or "worldly engagement". 

This divinatio functions as an adapter between you and the worldly activity modeled therein. 

>*Example:* An adapter `divinatio` to the Medical Oncologist version of myself consists of a message from that version, providing advice about whatever I'm dealing with in the moment.
