1. **MIND AND REALITY.** What is the underlying structure of the human mind, of my mind in particular, of external reality, and the interface between? (`phi`, `sys`)
	1. What is human nature?
	2. How do human minds process and understand the universe in all its heterogeneity, and distinguish sense from nonsense, the signal from the noise, in the seemingly overwhelming complexity of experience?
	3. To what extent can I construct my particular mind as a simultaneously [high-performing operating system](<Subjective Computation>) as well as a generator of timeless, spirituophilosophical wisdom?

2. **MORALITY.** What is right, good and just? (`phi`, `sys`)
	1. `coro:` How should I organize my life around what is right and good?
	2. `ext:` What is justice? How should we organize society around what is just; how should we discover what justice is, what our obligations are to each other, and how we can feasibly fulfill these demands? What is fairness? Can we realize fairness?

3. **SIMULATION.** Is it possible to simulate natural phenomena using formal, computational or artificial systems? What are the limitations of formal systems in simulating natural, emergent properties in nature? (`cs`, `sys`)
	- Equivalent: does there exist a procedure for generating organic phenomena? (Procedural generation) `gen` Does there exist a finitary procedure for generating languages, medical specialties, organisms, universes, mythologies/stories?

4. **END DISEASE.** What are the systems we need to implement at a global and local level that are necessary and sufficient to end all disease? (`phi`, `med`, `sys`)

5. **THEORY OF EVERYTHING.** Does there exist a finite and feasibly learnable set of axioms, foundations, theories, practices, and belief systems that can sufficiently explain all of reality? In other words, is there a theory or family of theories of everything? What is the nature of this theory? (`phi`, `sys`)

6. **EMERGENCE.** What are the processes and rules by which higher levels of order emerge from lower levels, of emergent properties irreducible to interactions of their components? (`sys`, `med`, `phi`)
	1. `equiv:` How do pattern, stability, and order emerge from chaos in systems of interacting components? What are the laws governing this emergence?

7. **LIMITS OF UNDERSTANDING.** What is the human mind capable of understanding? What are its limitations? (`phi`, `cs`)
	1. `equiv:` what is the event horizon of human comprehension, and how far can we extend our understanding past this frontier to understand the world (at least approximately) as it really is?

8. **PERSUASION AND RHETORIC.** What does excellent persuasive and galvanic rhetoric and communication look like, in public and private? (`phi`)

9. **GROUNDS OF BELIEF SYSTEMS.** What are the causes, grounds, and conditions that promote the propagation of different kinds of belief systems? How can we measure, model, simulate, and understand this propagation? (`phi`, `sys`)

10. **STRUCTUREBENDING.** Given any structure, institution, theory, piece of information, or idea, how can I hijack that idea and bend it to my will? (`phi`,`sys`)
	1. `[institution / structure] STRUCTUREBENDING := ` how can I hijack institutions and bend them to my will?
	2. `meta:` we borrow Harper's notation for substitution into `abts`.

11. **POWERFUL FORCES.** What is the nature of the most powerful forces that influence my life?
	1. The nature of social influence?
	2. The nature of emotion?
	3. The nature of political institutions?
	4. The nature of technology?
	5. The nature of human beings?

12. **SHEPHERD.** How do we shepherd our grand dreams into this world? How do we bridge the chasm between idea and implementation?
	1. What is the nature of the relationship betweeen abstractions and objects in the world?
