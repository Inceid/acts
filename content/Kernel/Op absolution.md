# Notation 
\<idea sequence\>
`abs` \<absolute extension or completion of the idea sequence\>

*aliases:*
- `complete` or *completion*
- `hypergen` or *hypergeneralization*

# Definition 
**Absolution** transforms and returns the absolute version, logical conclusion, or maximal generalization (also called a “hypergeneralization”) of its input idea or idea sequence.
