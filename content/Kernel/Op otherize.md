# Notation. 
`otherize` \<message from `Stranger` or some other objective observer of your situation\>

# Definition.
**Otherizing** yourself refers to taking the perspective of a stranger or observer unfamiliar with the particulars of your mind and thought, but able to reason from your perspective about what actions to take for the future.
