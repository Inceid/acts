# Notation 
`inv` \<thoughtseq\>

# Definition
**Inversion** inverts a component or the overall valence of a thought sequence/stream. 

# Examples and Motivation
>*Example 1.* Vicious thought-cycles.

Consider a vicious negative cycle of self-deprecating thoughts. 

The inversion of these loops consists of the following: 

>Your rumination signals that you have an enormously powerful mind. It takes a [valence](<Valence>) and rapidly builds thoughts that reinforce that valence. That’s a massive advantage - the only problem is that the valence that you’re feeding into is negative. It’s a self-destructive valence. To solve this cycle, you don’t have to change how your mind works. You just have to change the valence. That takes a lot of work, but it’s completely doable.

then followed by mindset inversions applied to negative thought loops as they occur in the moment. 

>*Example 2.* Existential crises.

Consider any large-scale existential problem. 
1. Climate change stands to eradicate human civilization if we do not act fast; it will already do serious permanent damage to the global economy due to destabilization of livable coastal lands, creating a massive refugee crisis. 
2. Political strife among nations will increase the risk of nuclear warfare manyfold; it is unclear if humanity can survive a nuclear catastrophe, the likelihood of which is increasing every year.
3. Every developed country has a rapidly aging population, which will create a demand/undersupply crisis for the healthcare system. It is unclear what we will be able to do as a society to take care of all of our elderly and chronically ill members. 

On one hand, these are genuinely terrifying to think about. 

On the other hand, per an inversion, they are fascinating and exciting as grand challenges for our species. In so many ways and from so many different directions we have been issued (or issued ourselves) a death sentence. But what if we found a way to claim life from the jaws of annihilation? Wouldn't that be cool? Wouldn't that be the thing that is the most important possible thing you could do today, to figure out how to claim life and harmonious existence for humanity from a nigthmarish, annihilative future? 

Perhaps the inversion isn't convincing to others due to its improbability - it is to me, simply because the resultant obligation - to put all of my energy on solving the world's greatest problems and find people willing to do the same - is cool. It's not boring. It's something I can motivate myself to do on a regular basis.
