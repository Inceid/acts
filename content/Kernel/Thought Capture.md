# Preamble
>What does it mean, to be able to write at the speed of thought, to think at the speed of writing, without distraction and diffraction? 

# Definition 
**Thought capture** refers to any technique that “captures” thought, focuses it, or renders it into a form sensible or describable by another being. 

“Another being” can be you at another point in time or another person proper. 

# Implementation
Thought capture allows me to navigate my thoughts and build them out into actionable frameworks for communication and decision making. 

I implement thought capture via two reflective systems: [Republic Internum in Obsidian](<What is Republic Internum>) and [[The Reflectorate]], my network of physical notebooks.

See details in [[Designing a Device Suprastructure|supra]].
