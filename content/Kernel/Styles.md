# Definition
A **style** is a loose way of doing things. The thing could be writing, talking, thinking, acting, or being (or anything else that there can be a particular way of doing).

# Index
## Substance-First Style
* `purpose` to get ideas, initial versions, or first-drafts onto the page
* `description` rather than agonizing over form, choose substance first. Get whatever thoughts you have onto an error-permissive non-performative medium

## Adaptive Style
* `purpose` polymerize a thoughtstream at a low threshold; add a substructure to your thought or inner dimension
* `description` rather than trying to install or familiarize yourself with an alternate or foreign system, adapt to the system you currently have, and extend it as needed.
* `example` in response to a culture a [*martial tempo*](<Martial Tempo.md>)


## Expansion-Restriction
* `purpose` swap between different styles of idea selection and proliferation.
* `description` 
	* `expansion` take an idea and expand it, adopt it, work with it. promote all possible uses of the idea. 
	* `restriction` take an idea and restrict it to its fundamentals. do not accept additional ideas. minimalize it. promote only the uses that advance its narrow purposes.
* `example` in response to learning or upskilling in AI, you might think 'I don't want to have to learn how to do this, it's not in my wheelhouse, I don't want to be the AI guy'. These are all restrictive patterns. In some settings this may be an advantageous thought pattern, but when you are a student (medical student, grad student, etc.), it is almost always beneficial to adopt an expansive pattern towards new emerging technologies. Consider the following expansive inversions: 'Learning how to do this makes me a better analyst and more flexible; it can become a tool in my toolkit; I don't have to be the AI guy, I can be the AI-Biosec-LGBTQ+-philosophy guy and have this as one of many tools.'

## Visual Style
* `purpose` when trying to render an idea visceral or concrete, visualize and animate it in your head

## Recursive Style
* `purpose` when trying to exemplify how an idea works in unfamiliar or interesting circumstances
* `description` when explicating a principle, apply it to itself.
* `source` Visual Explanations, GEB

## Transcriptive Style
* `purpose` to consolidate & harmonize content from multiple platforms into a single one
* `description` copy, paste.

## Dialogical Style
* `purpose` to engage with one’s thoughts and ideas conversationally, simulating their natural progression and evolution.

## Oscillating Style
- `description` To think, speak, and write in an oscillating pattern that alternates rapidly between two or more extreme values of some quality. 

## Nonlinear Style
* `description` To think across obvious/logical/deductive boundaries among disciplines. 

## Combinatorioselective
* `description` To think, speak, and write by permuting and generating many unique combinations of ideas followed by a selection of the ideas that best satisfy a set of filters or criteria.

## Visceral Style
- `description` To write using exacting, overwhelming, tidal, vast, colossal terms that convey a sense of grandeur.

## Computational Style
- `description` To write like a computer or algorithm.

## [[Accumulative Style]]
- `description` To think, speak, write, learn, and experience by always integrating and adding onto prior experience and knowledge.

## [[Analog-first style]]
- `description` To think, speak, and write first by doing so in an analog (as opposed to digital) format.
