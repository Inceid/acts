Develop a consistent routine of opening your slip-box whenever you're reading through a book. Grab the same notebooks every day for projects you're working on, and the positive feedback effects of using a slip-box as external scaffolding will become readily apparent. 

As we’re creatures of habit, the best way to start doing these things is to just start doing them consistently in some shape or form, and refine as you go.
