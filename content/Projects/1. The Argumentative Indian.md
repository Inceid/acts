- "A defeated argument that refuses to be obliterated can remain very alive." (6)
- Early Buddhist and Jain literature contained justifications and expositions of protest and resistance against the dominant priestly class of the period - it was by nature political and egalitarian. (10)
- The force of the most powerful arguments in Indian intellectual history comes from the substantive force of their claims rather than on formal style of "cultivated/well-trained brilliance". (12)
- The flourishing of democracy in India is often attributed to the British, despite the fact that they conquered 100+ other countries and many of them did not adopt democracy after liberation. (12)
- **A tradition of public reasoning with tolerance for heterodoxy has strong links to democracy across the globe**.
- "Democracy is intimately concerned with public discussion and *interactive reasoning*." (13)
