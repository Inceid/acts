>There's no fine line between work and play.

# Preamble 
This essay describes the core values of my work ethic. 

# Fun and Efficiency
I optimize for two things in my learning and work: fun and efficiency. 

If something is not fun, I make it as efficient as possible to get it out of the way. If it can't get any more efficient than it is, I try to make it fun or interesting. 

Fun is more important than efficiency. If the way I study a topic is extremely fun but inefficient, I won't change how I study it. Life is too short to waste time doing stuff that makes me miserable. 

Thankfully, usually making things more efficient is itself fun for me (at least to a point).
