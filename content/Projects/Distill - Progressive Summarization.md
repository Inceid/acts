Pitfalls to avoid:
1. **Over-highlighting** - use an upper threshold of 20%
2. **Highlighting without purpose** - highlight when you're getting ready to *create something*
3. **Making highlighting difficult** - use your gut to determine whether you should highlight something. Don't overthink or obsessively categorize each point to determine whether you should highlight it. You can apply the same capture criteria as in Ch. 4 as well.
