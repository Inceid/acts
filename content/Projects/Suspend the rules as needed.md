[These rules](<Timebox your day.md>) exist as aids for bringing you to points where you can discover deep, extraordinary insights and build magnificently valuable things. If you find yourself in a spot where it’s better to suspend these rules to achieve that, go ahead and do it. 

>Example: in the middle of a 2-hour programming block, you find yourself getting lost in the flow and discovering something awesome. But the block is almost over! Do you stick to your blocks or keep going until your deep dive loses steam?

In these situations, better to trust your gut and suspend the rules until you lose steam, then regroup and reschedule. 

The key is **comprehensive adaptive scheduling**. Comprehensiveness in scheduling keeps you from regressing to shallow tasks; adaptiveness allows you to shift priorities and lean into spontaneity when it arises. Implemented this way, **structure enables spontaneity**, rather than stifling it. 

The overall purpose here is to establish an approach that treats your time with respect, as only then will you have a chance of reaching your full potential. Perhaps paradoxically, you can only respect your time if you spend some of it to allocate the rest of it with intention.
