`meta`
- for funsies, I use a more complex system than PARA for organization - mine is inspired by a Kernel and Council model. We started work on this well before our BASB reading, but BASB will augment our structural expansion of `RI`. (10-30-24)
- the interface of the Kernel/Council governance model is a set of four folders and an "Inbox". The Inbox is not a folder - it's the empty space in the overall universe. The four primary folders are "External", "Journal", "Kernel", and "Projects", each reflecting the primary intellectual activities I want to engage in throughout my life. I want to engage with the external world through conversation and observation and capture the most salient elements of those experiences; I want to write about the free-form streams of thought running through my mind; I want to complete projects across a vast breadth of fields; and, I want to build a complex but functional (and beautiful!) theory of mind that I can operationalize and modify to achieve each of the preceding goals. Those goals are represented in the five-part interface of `RI`. (12-28-24)
