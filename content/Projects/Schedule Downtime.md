It is extremely important to schedule a "shutdown" time during each workday after which point you completely stop thinking about work and allow your mind to be idle. 

Reasons: 
1. Downtime allows your unconscious mind to process ambiguous patterns and large swathes of information in ways your conscious mind cannot. 

2. Downtime gives your conscious mind time to recharge its directed attention pool. To that end, low-stimulation activities like walks in nature replenish directed attention. This effect is independent of whether activity in nature improves mood - any engagement with nature, comforting or discomforting, appears to replenish attention resources. 

>You can restore your ability to direct your attention if you give it a chance to rest. 

3. The work that downtime replaces isn’t usually that important.
