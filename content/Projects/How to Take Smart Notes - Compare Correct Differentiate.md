# Compare, correct, and differentiate 
Splaying multiple notes out in front of us allows us to *perceive* rather than imagine or implicitly interpret differences between ideas. 

Drawing comparisons, differences, harmonies and oppositions between ideas in a manner that spans disciplines is critical to generating and refining unique ideas. 

It also forces us to confront ourselves and revise our old thinking, as adding a new note may yield a contradiction with an older note, upon which one or the other has to be modified to harmonize the overall line of thought. 

Comparison and contrast with old notes also helps distinguish long-term lines of thought from short-term excursions. 

This is critical to tracking the development of larger arguments, hypotheses, and models in one’s thought over time.
