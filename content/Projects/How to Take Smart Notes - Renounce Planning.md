### Become an expert, not a planner 
Analytical rationality must be paired with embodied practice, speed, and an intimate knowledge of concrete examples to attain expertise. 

"To be able to become an expert, we need the freedom to make our own decisions and all the necessary mistakes that help us learn." (65)

"Teachers tend to mistake the ability to follow (their) rules with the ability to make the right choices in real situations." (66)
