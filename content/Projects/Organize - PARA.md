# Project
Short term efforts in your work or life that you're working on *now*.
# Areas
Long-term responsibilities you want to manage over time.
# Resource
Topics or interests that may be useful in the future.
# Archive
Inactive items from any of the other three categories.

`portal:` [Smart Notes](<How to Take Smart Notes - Simplicity is Paramount>) `:` similar to the fleeting, permanent, and project framework - the mapping is not 1-1, as fleeting/permanent are unprocessed/processed versions of notes for any of the first three areas.
