# Primordia and Motivation
It's clear that I want to learn about a shit ton of stuff, abstract, concrete, and everything in between. And I understand that the demands on my time from the external world will only become steeper.

# Definition
The **Syllabus Vitae** is a summative document that describes essential readings, conversations, opportunities, and practices that effectively operationalizes our goals of lifelong learning.

# Usage
The Syllabus Vitae is intended to be used as a summative agenda and strategic plan for my high-priority inquiries. 

# Implementatiopn
- Description: include a basic description of the purpose and structure of the SV.

- General Approach: include the general principles for designing the SV. 

- Major components:
1. Allocation: Top-down/Global, Bottom-up/Local, Meso/Semiglobal. 
2. Processing: [[Current Active Cycle]] and [Queues](<Queued Civs.md>).
3. Combination/Termination: 
* **modularization** of processes into checkpoints and termination upon each checkpoint with a **Hemingway Bridge** to the next session.
- **deliverablization** of understanding prerequisites into two types of deliverables: projects and summary sheets. 
	- A summary sheet is a summary of concepts covered in a course in your own words. It demonstrates understanding of the relevant theory behind those concepts. 
	- A project is a functioning, evergreen implementation of the concepts covered in a course. It can be produced with guidance from a syllabus or outline, but must be completed by you.
