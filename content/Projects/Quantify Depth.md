Sometimes, it’s not obvious whether a task is shallow or deep. In reality, tasks fall on a spectrum somewhere between the extremes. To determine where a task falls on the scale, ask: 

>How long would it take (in months) to train a smart recent college grad, with no specialized training in my field, to do this? 

A higher number indicates higher prerequisite levels of expertise, indicating a task on the deeper side of the spectrum.
