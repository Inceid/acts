Add your permanent notes into the slip-box in a consistent routine.

The basic idea: after you finish a permanent note, add it into your slip-box in the appropriate compartment, link to other notes as you see appropriate, then link it to an index so that it can be easily accessed globally.

`meta:` note the subtlety in recommending global *and* local access. Global access ensures that we do not lose the note in the maze of ideas in our box (Vault in our case). Local access promotes discovery of associations after we've forgetten about the note via navigating through the slip-box by links (Graph View in our case).
