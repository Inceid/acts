# Speaker: TSI 
Anxiety about the world is a call to action. 

Action is its only antidote. 

Once you act, you will find that you want to act more. Action begets action. It is its own positive feedback loop. Inaction begets inaction. 

Anxiety about an overload of tasks is a call for a systematic approach. If you don’t have a system, design and validate one. If your system isn’t working, adjust it accordingly.
