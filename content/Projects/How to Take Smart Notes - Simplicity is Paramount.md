>You’ll only benefit from the slip-box by modifying *your whole workflow* to adapt to it.

There are three types of notes: fleeting, permanent, and project. 
- Fleeting notes capture your important thoughts in the moment. 
- Permanent notes create a reservoir to develop major ideas over a long period. 
- Project notes operationalize permanent notes to complete a specific project. 

# Typical pitfalls in note frameworks 
- Trying to record every idea as a permanent idea 
- Trying to enforce a strict linear ordering on collected ideas 
- Trying to collect ideas only related to specific projects 
- Indiscriminately recording fleeting notes with no relationships to other notes 
- Leaving a fleeting note unprocessed, then forgetting its motivation/context. 

# Permanent Notes
Permanent notes can be reference notes or generative notes. The latter are self-contained ideas in your own words.
