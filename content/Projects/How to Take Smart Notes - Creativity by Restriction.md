# Exploit standardization
Exploit standardization as a creative, generative tool in the following ways:
1. Length: as a rule of thumb, each note should fit on the screen with no need to scroll.
2. Process: Connect notes in the same way each time. Alleviating the technicalities of notetaking allows you to focus on what matters: the ideas themselves.
3. Restriction 
>“Not having to make choices can unleash a lot of potential, which would otherwise be wasted on making these choices.” (p. 130) 

Creativity flourishes under restriction. Consider Haikus: their format doesn’t restrict their expressiveness, and arguably allows them to transcend culture. 

>[!key] Creativity through Structure
A clear structure, standardized processes, and restrictive rules of operation *promote* creativity and scientific progress. (p. 131)
