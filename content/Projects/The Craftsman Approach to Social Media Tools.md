>**The Craftsman Approach:** identify the most fundamental factors determining success in your life. Adopt a tool only if its positive impacts on these factors substantially outweighs its drawbacks.

To determine those fundamental factors, take some time and write out each major personal/professional goal you have. Then add a list of key activities that support this goal. 

>*Example:*
	**Personal Goal:** Complete a personal programming language and computer system. 
	Key activities: 
	- Read and annotate educational resources.
	- Work on small-to-medium sized deliverables. 

Note that the key activities are activities in the proper sense, not outcomes or other goals. They are general acts that you should commit to doing regularly.

Do that for all your major goals. That’s your list of fundamental factors. For my case, given my goal and activities above, I would systematically eliminate my usage of any platforms that fail the craftsman criterion. 
	→ No X/Twitter, no Instagram, no Facebook. 
	→ Yes YouTube, but only videos that explain things better than written sources.
