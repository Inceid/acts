Schedule your internet use during the day, and *do not use it* otherwise. 

>Initial step: write down on a notepad the next time you’re allowed to use the internet.

Idea: Using a distracting service doesn’t kill your focus. It’s the constant *switching* between low-stimulation/high-value and high-stimulation/low-value activities that weakens your mind’s ability to mediate your attention. 

Note that this strategy still allows you to use the internet a lot! Totally fine if you need it for your job or personal engagements. The point is to schedule it, and abide by that schedule.
