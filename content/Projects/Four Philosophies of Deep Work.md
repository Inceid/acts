*The Monastic*. Practitioners organize their life to maximize long stretches of deep concentrated thought and minimize their engagement with any shallow commitments.

*The Bimodal*. Practioners dedicate multiple-days-chunks of time to working on deep projects and leave the rest of their time open for shallow commitments. This is well-suited to those who cannot avoid engaging with shallower commitments for a substantial portion of their professional time.

*The Rhythmic*. Practitioners implement deep work as a regular habit done in shorter stretches every day at regular intervals. 

*The Journalist*. Practitioners implement deep work by engaging in it whenever they have extra time, in the moment.
