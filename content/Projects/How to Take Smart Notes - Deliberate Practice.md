Learning requires deliberate practice with self-testing via retrieval practice and elaboration.

Deliberate practice is rendered possible by the slip-box because the box takes care of details and references by itself. It keeps information unaltered, and allows the user to mix and reassociate notes at will. 

That frees your brain up to focus on the **gist** of a topic, to gain deeper understanding and a bigger picture of what it’s looking at. It frees your brain up to be creative.
