Our motivation and willpower is an extremely limited resource. To best direct it towards decisions that matter, we must have a consistent working environment and consistent **simplified** working system, wherein we process quick notes into permanent notes and project notes in the same way each time.

Doing so allows us to pick up and put down our work without losing momentum. This enables breaks, which are crucial for the brain to process information in its background cognition.
