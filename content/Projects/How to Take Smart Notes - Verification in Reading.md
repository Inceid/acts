Permanent notes for a text should be written as if to an audience with only a general knowledge of the field and no knowledge of the context or points of the text. That audience is your future selves. 
`portal` [BASB Ch. 3 p. 47](<Annotation of Building a Second Brain#Chapter 3 How a Second Brain Works>)

While you read, it is critical to **verify** your understanding of what you're reading by writing and rephrasing in your terms. This simulates active retrieval. 

Understanding is also verified when you try to craft literature notes, and again when you turn literature notes into permanent notes. 

If the resulting summary or commentary makes no sense or is missing key points, that probably means you need to go back to the text.
