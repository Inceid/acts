If you find you’re rescheduling your time blocks too often, consider applying these tactics: 

### Anticipate underestimation. 
You should anticipate that most tasks will take a little longer than you first think they will to complete. Build this pessimism into your scheduling. 

### Overflow conditional blocks. 
Suppose you’re not sure how long it will take to finish your Biology homework. Then block off the expected time - say 90 minutes - and add an extra “conditional block” afterwards with a split purpose:
* If you need more time to finish the homework, take it. 
- Otherwise, have an alternate use ready (answering email, reading a book, studying math).

### Use blocks liberally. 
Schedule lots of task blocks throughout the day, and make them longer than you think they should be. Group more them in the mornings while you have more energy and time to speak for.
