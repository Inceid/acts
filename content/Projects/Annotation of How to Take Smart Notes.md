# The Six Steps to Successful Writing 
## 1. Separate and Interlocking Tasks
>Standardize your workflow to minimize loss of attention, working memory, and willpower.
- [Spend Your Attention Wisely](<How to Take Smart Notes - Do not multitask.md>)
- [Become an Expert, not a Planner](<How to Take Smart Notes - Renounce Planning.md>)
- [Extend Your Memory via Closure](<How to Take Smart Notes - Closure Extends Memory.md>)
- [Reduce the Number of Decisions](<How to Take Smart Notes - Simplify.md>)

## 2. Reading for Understanding 
>Write as you read with a charitable attitude and attention to important information.
- [Write as you read](<How to Take Smart Notes - Taking Literature Notes.md>)
- [Keep an Open Mind](<How to Take Smart Notes - Disconfirmation.md>) 
- [Get the gist](<How to Take Smart Notes - Getting the Main Idea.md>)
- [Learn to Read](<How to Take Smart Notes - Verification in Reading.md>)
- [Learn by Reading](<How to Take Smart Notes - Deliberate Practice.md>)

## 3. Take Smart Notes 
>Restate ideas in your words, ask questions about them, and draw connections.
- [Restate Ideas, Ask Questions](<How to Take Smart Notes - Restate and Inquire.md>)
- [Learn by Not Trying](<How to Take Smart Notes - Connections as Retrieval.md>)
- [Use Permanent Notes](<How to Take Smart Notes - Add Permanent Notes.md>)

## 4. Develop Ideas 
> Connect, compare, and contrast notes. Assemble a broad lattice of theories. Exploit restriction.
- [Develop Topics](<How to Take Smart Notes - Develop Topics>)
- [Make Smart Connections](<How to Take Smart Notes - Connect Your Notes.md>)
- [Draw Comparisons](<How to Take Smart Notes - Compare Correct Differentiate>)
- [Assemble Tools for Thinking](<How to Take Smart Notes - A Broad Toolbox.md>)
- [The Slip-box as a Creativity Machine](<How to Take Smart Notes - Creativity by Connection.md>)
- [Think Inside the Box](<How to Take Smart Notes - Abstraction, Feedback, Debiasing.md>)
- [Facilitate Creativity Through Restriction](<How to Take Smart Notes - Creativity by Restriction.md>)

## 5. Share your Insight 
>Start projects bottom up from clusters. Follow your interests. Iterate and interleave projects.
- [Build Bottom Up, Iterate, Variate](<How to Take Smart Notes - Bottom Up Structure, Iteration, Variation.md>)

## 6. Make it a Habit
>Start today and establish a routine of [writing-as-you-read](Syngenesis) practices.
- [Make it a Habit](<How to Take Smart Notes - Making the Slip-Box a Habit.md>)
