# Definition
**Timeboxing** is a technique whereby you take the hours of your day and break them into blocks of length 30 minutes to 2 hours, with each block dedicated to a focused task. 

# Implementation
For short/shallow tasks like answering emails or submitting forms, you can box them into a general “Tasks” block and write out all of the small tasks next to the block. 

Be flexible in adapting your timeboxing to circumstances. If your schedule is disrupted or tasks take longer than usual, that’s okay! Make a note of that and reschedule your remaining hours accordingly. *The goal is not to stick to one schedule, but to maintain a say in how you’re using your time in real time*.
