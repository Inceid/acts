**Disconfirmatory reasoning** tests ideas against opposing views. 

It is essential for developing a project for revealing objective insights. 

>"If insight becomes a threat to your academic or writing success, you are doing it wrong." (p. 80) 

The slip-box, by forcing us to restate main ideas, considering their relevance, *then* synthesizing them into conclusions, enables disconfirmatory thinking. Disconfirming data is attractive for box-users, because it creates more possible conversations in the box!

# Prioritize highly connected notes as projects
To apply disconfirmation to project selection, we can shift our attention away from specific pre-conceived projects to clusters of connections within the slip-box. 

We select ideas from readings that are *relevant* (supporting and opposing) to clusters and conversations in the box.
