In the case of Bell Labs, it was a *hub and spoke* architecture that optimized both for collaboration and sustained reflection. 

Private offices were private, and doors could be closed for extended periods of private work. But all of these offices - the “spokes” - were connected with a “hub”, like the center of a wheel - the long hallways - such that scientists would *have* to interact with one another when traveling from place to place. 

Both of these approaches are attempts to recognize and capture the value of collaboration in realizing the value of deep work. 

One way to do this well is by triggering the *whiteboard effect*, which occurs when rapid back-and-forth between two people pushes both parties to think more deeply than either would have in isolation. 

Bottom line: collaboration, when appropriate and juxtaposed with sustained periods of focused reflection, can bolster deep work.
