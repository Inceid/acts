Thinking within the rules of the slip-box promotes creativity. Here’s how:

# Abstraction 
“To be able to play with ideas, we first have to liberate them from their original context by means of abstraction and re-specification.”
Abstraction is necessary to make heterogeneous ideas compatible. It is also necessary for drawing effective analogies, comparisons, and combinations.

# “The 5 Elements of Effective Thinking”
In this book, the authors describe some strategies that are already implemented in the slip-box, and others to keep in mind:
1. Prioritize fast feedback loops: confront yourself with errors, mistakes, misunderstandings. 
	- doing this is an extremely effective way to refine your thinking. Avoiding this will likely waste lots of time in repeated failure. 
	- `portal` [[Rapid Research]]
2. Focus on the main ideas behind details: get the gist. 
3. Describe observations as plainly and factually as possible (do not add premature interpretation).
	- be aware of possible biases in your descriptions as you do so, and revise the descriptions accordingly.
	- pay close attention to what you *don’t* see or observe - what’s missing?
4. Take simple ideas *extremely* seriously.
	- many times simple principles are what mediate complicated-looking processes.
