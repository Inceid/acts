>A **grand gesture** is a radical change in one’s normal environment and life, usually coupled with a significant cost or investment of some kind.

Grand gestures convince your mind of the importance of a task, reducing its instinct to procrastinate. As such, they can decrease the activation energy to starting/reinforcing an important task. 

Any gesture or commitment that forces one’s mind to commit extremely seriously to the task at hand works well for this purpose.
