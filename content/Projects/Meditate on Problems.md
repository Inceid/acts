Take some periods while you’re occupied doing something that’s low-stimulation - running, jogging, driving, showering - and focus on a [complex or difficult problem](<Positive Rumination>). Try to make some progress on it. Two key tactics to improve success here:

1. Recognize and point out when you **loop** over the same ideas. Try to figure out an escape from the loop, the next concrete step.
2. Structure your meditations. 
	- Identify the variables for solving a problem and store them in your memory. 
	- Define a next-step question to answer using these variables. 
	- Try and verify solutions. 
	- Consolidate your gains by reviewing the answer that works. (If you keep choosing an answer that doesn’t work, explain to yourself why you choose it and why it doesn’t work.)

All of these tactics take practice and time to develop! The key is to challenge your capacity for tolerating and training through boredom each time. You’ll get better as you keep doing it.
