# Preamble 
We need a summative document representative of our “true” curriculum and achievements. 

# Motivation
Typical CVs tend to conform to practices that limit their potential to express underlying intellectual talent and work.
- Many CVs highlight or privilege papers and manuscripts over oral talks, seminars, teaching exercises. But there is no evidence to suggest that manuscripts are more impactful than other academic exercises that generate value.
- Colloquially, the value of a CV is thought to be a function of its length. This is unjustified. A CV can be long and full of nonsense. The value of an intellectual contribution should be a function of its novelty, soundness, and generalizability. The CVV is my attempt to create a document that exemplifies these values.

# Definition 
The **Curriculum Vitae Verus** is a compact summative document containing one’s major academic, intellectual, and personal achievements. 

# Basic Structure
The basic structure of a CVV should include the following:

## Identifiers
>An identifier is any word or phrase that distinguishes you.

Standard identifiers include name, contact information, platforms that house your work, and any other relevant personal/professional/intellectual identifiers.

## Background
>A background should rapidly orient readers to your skills and knowledge base.

Standard background identifiers include
- Educational background
- Ongoing research

## Portfolio
>A portolio is a curated list of works representative of the style and quality of the author of those works.

Standard portfolio elements include:
- Deliverables from ongoing research

## Founts
>All of our ideas are derived in some sense from others. Some are derived more heavily from certain sources than others. 

Include here any external sources that function as persistent inspirations for further projects and ideas.
