##### `pri`
* p. 223-224
	* The tools of our trade have become abstract and immaterial: the building blocks of ideas, insights, facts, frameworks, and mental models. 
	* Your fears, doubts, mistakes, missteps, failures, and self-criticism - it's all just information to be taken in, processed, and made sense of.
	* Navigating the terrain requires shifting our mindset towards information in the following ways:
		* From toolset to mindset
		* From scarcity to abundance
		* From obligation to service
		* From consuming to creating
	* "I often hear that people start to feel a tremendous sense of conviction - for their goals, their dreams, and the things they want to change or influence in the world - because they know they have a powerful system behind them amplifying every move they make."
		* `meta:` conviction is not inside yourself nor outside yourself but in the boundary between. It is found in decisions you make to warp the external world so that it represents your internal world. It is found in the systems you design to dialogue between your worlds. The boundary between.
			* Everyone who tells you you're going to somehow find yourself is fucking lying or lazy. Things like wisdom and purpose and fulfillment are not found in here, they're not found out there, they're not found anywhere. They're forged. 
* p. 230
	* "You don't need to go out and hunt down insights. All you have to do is listen to what life is repeatedly trying to tell you."
	* Give up low-value work that gives you a false sense of security but that doesn't call forth your higher self. Let go of low-value information. 
* p. 231-232
	* The purpose of knowledge is to be shared. What's the point of knowing something if it doesn't have a positive impact on someone, not even yourself? If I share my knowledge, that makes it more valuable!
	* "Your singular perspective may patch some small hole in the vast tattered fabric of humanity." *The Bullet Journal Method*
* p. 233-234
	* Do not over-intellectualize: self-knowledge begins with what resonates. Only what resonates will transform into a true Second Brain, a Republic Internum, an internal civilization that reflects and magnifies the power of the universe. 
	* There is a universe of thoughts and ideas and emotions within you.
* p. 236
	* The world is desperate to hear what you know.
