# Chapter 1: Introduction.
Challenges typically ask you to go beyond the scope of the chapter in order to gain a deeper understanding of the material; they are not required.

Some challenges ask you to modify your interpreter. If you do so, make sure to make an unmodified copy, as later chapters assume a vanilla version.
