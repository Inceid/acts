# Get the gist
The ability to extract the **gist**, the main idea, of a text, is an intellectual’s bread and butter. The more often one does it and the more focused one is, the better they become. 

Key to this is distinguishing important from less important parts of a text. 

The best way to get good at this is by doing it deliberatively, using your own judgment to distinguish important parts of a text, and to restate the gist in your terms.

>"Rewriting what was already written almost automatically trains one to shift the attention towards *frames, patterns, and categories* in the observations, or the *conditions/assumptions*, which enable certain, but not other descriptions." 
>- *Luhmann* (p. 84)
