# Think outside the text
Experienced readers think *beyond the frames and questions in a given text*, relating its assumptions, inquiries and arguments to unstated ones. Less experienced readers adopt and work exclusively within the frames of a text. 

To be able to understand the meaning of information in a text to apply that information beyond its context, write concise restatements of points of a text instead of collecting quotes. Then draw connections between those ideas and ideas from other contexts. 

# Think outside the brain 
In order for your notes and the texts they describe to advance your inquiries, you have to ask questions about them. 

*Put these questions in your notes!* 

And look in your slip-box for possible leads before consulting additional literature.
