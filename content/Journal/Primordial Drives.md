# Definition
**Primordial Drives** are the lowest-level drives that motivate me to do all the things that I do in a day. 

These are elaborated into [[Constitution|Design Principles]] for `RI` and the kernel. 

# Curiosity
The urge to explore, ask questions, and seek new knowledge.

# Wonder
The sense of marvel and fascination at the world’s mysteries.

# Awe
The feeling of humility and reverence in the face of something vast or profound.

# Theorycraft / Ad Hoc Formalism
The love of of building models, frameworks, and systems to make sense of complexity.

# Understanding
The desire to grasp underlying principles, patterns, and connections.

# Transcendence
The urge to go beyond the ordinary and connect with something greater.

# Creation
The drive to bring something new into existence and express inner worlds.

# Excellence / Prowess / Mastery
The intrinsic motivation to master skills, refine abilities, and pursue personal bests.
