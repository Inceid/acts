>Analogies are fundamental to human reasoning. But nobody stops and asks - what are they?

# Definition
An **analogy** is a mapping between two spaces that communicates a similarity, relationship, or common pattern between them.

An **analogy** is an bijective mapping between two discrete spaces that preserves functional relations between them. It may also be called an **isomorphism**.
