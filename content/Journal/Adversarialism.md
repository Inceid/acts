# Definition 
**Adversarialism** is a method of action that identifies an adversary or agent with a putatively opposing stance to one's own and acting to battle that agent. 

# Adversarialism in Psychology and Internal Governance 
To avoid confirmation bias and convincing oneself of delusional beliefs, it’s very important to adopt adversarial models of internal deliberation and [governance](<Internal Governance>).

Adversarialism can be taken too far. Arguably, depressive rumination is a dysregulated form of adversarialism: opposing thoughts stack and stack, overwhelming one’s ability to use them to any useful effect.
