# Definition 
**Staging** is a learning tactic that involves setting a structure to fit concepts into *before* learning or reviewing those concepts in detail. 

In other words, it involves designing an *a priori* preparative schema for a set of concepts one anticipates learning and fitting into that structure. 

# Motivation 
The motivation behind staging is the recognition that it’s more important to proactively anticipate and model information of interest *prior to* experiential engagement with it than simply engaging with it and brute-force updating your approach each time.
