>"It is a common observation that a regulator or controller is often much smaller and simpler than the system it controls. The governor of a steam engine, the pilot of an aircraft, or the thermostat in a house are all examples of small regulators controlling much larger systems."
>Ashby, W. R. (1956). _An Introduction to Cybernetics_.

# Definition
A **controller** is a system that autonomously and effectively manipulates the behavior of a system much larger and more complex than itself.
