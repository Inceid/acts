# Definition
**Oneiromorphosis** is the transformation or encapsulation of one dream or vision into another. 

# Motivation 
You don’t have to “sacrifice” the set of your dreams that you can’t act on today in order to move forward. It suffices to choose the set of dreams that yields the highest return on investment for your time and energy, then convert the others into the language(s) of the primary dream(s). 

All of your dreams can survive by this conversion. They will simply inhabit the space of the dominant dreams and be thus protected. Perhaps they can also be acted on at a later time.
