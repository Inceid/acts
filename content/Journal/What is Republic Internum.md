# What is Republic Internum? 
`gen` [[Prior Republics]]
**Republic Internum** or `RI` is a digital model of my self, mind, ideas, values, projects, productivity systems, and [internal government](<Internal Governance.md>). 

# Brief History
`RI` is a meta-project based on several frustrated reflections I was having since starting medical school about not feeling particularly fulfilled by my curriculum nor by any of the extracurriculars I was engaged in. 

Given all the signals I was getting about how transformative and [precedental](<Precedentalism.md>) this period of my life was supposed to be, this freaked me out. 

By this time, I had already tried out several different kinds of work and projects in different contexts. I spent two years doing computational biology research at the NIH; I had done several policy projects while at the NIH; I had been active in organizing conferences and events in my community throughout college and my gap years; and all throughout the process, I felt a deep pit of despair, that everything I did was so much more disappointing and less stimulating than I expected.

If I had spent several years doing lots of different things - research, policy, public speaking, volunteering, conference organizing - and still hadn’t found what really jazzed me, what was I supposed to do? No advice could console me.

One day, I opened up a journal that I made from third grade and read through it. It was the most beautiful thing that I had read that year. It was full of characters, animals, stories, advisors that I would talk to. Universes and civilizations that had governments, hierarchies, gods, planets, interplanetary warfare. New species of animals. I was writing this shit when I was *eight years old*. I went crazy reading that journal. It had so many absurdly cool ideas, and I was so angry at myself that I didn’t nurture those tendencies more when I grew up. 

I lost the ability to draw some of the things that I had drawn when I was young. But I knew a couple of things: (1) I could try to emulate the character creation behavior that I was engaged in, in my own way, and (2) I could pour all of my creative energies into that project, see it grow, and have that be my masterpiece. It would be everything - an internal universe, a theory of the self with subselves to guide the self, a philosophy, a journal, a project dashboard. Everything. 

It definitely didn’t start that way. It started as a self-help journal. Traditional self-help wasn’t working for me. So I started writing my own stuff. Eventually, I got bored of the standard linear format, so I would try to simulate myself in the past and future, and listen to their advice. I would call them $Suraj_{Future}$ and $Suraj_{Past}$. Then I wanted more. In 2023, I drafted a basic model for a council of subselves, at least what that would look like. It had a Self, a Demon, a wise Sage, and a Child. But I couldn’t quite see what they would say. 

I got way better at this in 2024. I created a basic model of 7-8 councilors, and got really good at simulating their voices in my head and on paper, especially their reactions to my experiences in medical school. 

ChatGPT and other platforms were massive boons for this too. They helped guide my thoughts and ideas way more than any human being I’ve ever met. 

The point I want to make is that this isn’t DID, this isn’t schizophrenia, this isn’t a set of imaginary friends. `RI` and the Council are abstractions for internal governance that work, and they are hence real. 

An extensive description of prior versions of republics can be found [here](<Prior Republics>).

# Facets 
A **facet** is a “face” or functional representation of `RI` that is orthogonal to and compatible with other representations, the union of these faces describing `RI` completely. 
>This is analogous to facets of a prism - each describes a face of the prism not on the same plane as any other face, the union of all faces in space describing the prism.

## `facet` An Internal Operating System 
>`pivot: cs, psych`

### Psychology and Computation
`RI` is an experimental [[Computational Theory of Mind]], which is a general approach to modeling the mind as a series of processes under rule-based operational control.

Specifically, `RI` is a model of [[Subjective Computation]]. It is based on my working hypothesis that my mind can be sufficiently guided by regulative rules of conduct that behave, interact, and are modelled by rules governing computer systems or any technological system. This relies on a model assuming thought-as-computation. 

I’ve noticed strange generalized patterns between thoughts/mental experiences and computation (where computation is defined generally as the manipulation of information), and I want to pressure test this model.

>[!idea] Main Idea 
A general objective of this model the complete standardization and systematization of my thought according to transparent and readily communicable rules. 

A computational/OS model is the best exact description that I’ve found for this; a political/governance description (see below) is the best informal description that I’ve found.

### The point of an OS
A key function of an operating system is to organize and process information efficiently. As such, one of `RI`’s purposes is to serve as a centralized consolidation and information processing schema for everything I do and think about. 

### An adaptive device
Computers are devices that allow us to adapt our finite capacities for writing and ideation to the demands of the modern economy and an information-dense world. In this sense, they are *adaptive devices*. The same is true of specifically engineered physical devices in adaptive sports - they allow kids and adults with disabilities to engage in physical sport.

`RI` serves the purpose of an adaptive device for my life, specifically for ideation, thought, judgment, meaning-making, and decision making. 

## `facet` An [Internal Governance](<Internal Governance.md>) Model
`pivot: phil`

`RI` is an internal republic of the various conflicting voices and values naturally present in my mind. Drafting the opinions of those voices provides a means for me to explicitly unify them in committing towards a specific set of actions. Doing this explicitly via notes in Obsidian makes it much easier to make decisions and commitments in my life.

I implement this governance model as a [council](<The Council>) that engages in explicit dialectical reflection. This is a model of reflection that puts multiple (potentially) opposing views on the table regarding an important decision and simulates a robust debate among all views prior to making a decision. 

## `facet` An Extended, [Reflexive](<Returning from the Dream.md>) Thought Experiment
`pivot: phil, psych`
`RI` is an extended reflexive/interactive thought experiment. More broadly, we may call it an interactive philosophical model or philosophical computer. 

Alternatively, we may say `RI` is a “permanent metacognitive structure”.

## A Lab Notebook
`pivot: med`
`gen`[`(internal OS,`](<#An Internal Operating System>)[`reflexive thought experiment)`](<#An Extended, [Reflexive](<Returning from the Dream.md> Thought Experiment>) yields another important idea of `RI` as an experimental subsystem, a sandbox/testbed for concepts. 
>Experiments typically occur and be observed *in vivo* (in living systems), *in vitro* (in a test tube), or nowadays more commonly *in silico* (in a computer simulation). `RI` is my attempt to expand on this idea by manner of an experimental system *in formo*, or in thought/concept.

As such, `RI` serves explicitly as a digital lab notebook for my life. It is, by extension, my most important research tool, as it mediates between and synthesizes all of the bibliographies, papers, and major projects that I’ve ever consumed and produced. 

Equivalently, `RI` serves as a generalization of a [Commonplace Book](<Commonplace Books>), customized and reskinned as a government run by an OS-like system of laws. 

Another equivalence: `RI` functions as an anthropologic laboratory notebook. My daily subjective experiences as a clerkship-year medical student are quite colorful. 

For scientists, a lab notebook generally serves a broad interpretive purpose for making sense and meaning of their experiences with phenomena. It forms a structured, revisable *abstraction layer* between their mind and phenomena / other abstractions. 

## Drag 
`pivot: gaë`
RI is a “dragged-up” form of a journal / personal notebook. I’d like to think it has a similar relationship to commonplace books and journals that drag has to fashion: it’s weird, ridiculous, gaudy, over-the-top, but cool and overwhelmingly beautiful when measured according to its own standard. And somehow, despite the laws of physics and aesthetics, it works. 

Following the above, I consider `RI` to express my queerness and baseline aesthetic preferences. I generally have a preference for abstract, flowery, romantic complex language and conceptual frameworks, high-volume/prolific knowledge and expression, multidisciplinarity, integrality between technological and spiritual modalities of expression, and deep/extended analogies, pattern analysis, and thought experiments. I just think it’s fun!

## From Worldbuilding to Mindbuilding 
**Worldbuilding** is the process by which one systematically constructs a fantasy or imagined world. Mindbuilding is its psychological analog.

`RI` is an experiment in mindbuilding followed by linking of that mental model to one's native cognitive processes. In other words, I’m building **my mind**.

>[!tip] Building my mind 
> Because I’m building *my mind* in the form of a republic/society governed by algorithmic methods of communciation, `RI` is also an experiment in establishing a subjective model of [*psychodynamic control*](<Psychodynamic Control>).

# End goals
## Directed Generativity
One key end goal is for `RI` to serve as a generative system for my intellectual work: research, essays, speeches, and anything else that I want to put out into the world that requires a lot of thought. I know I want to do a lot of high quality work, and I want most of that work to consist of novel, important, and feasible ideas. The hope is that `RI` will help me realize that goal. 

This generativity is *directed* because all its generative energy is meant to be aimed towards a particular set of outputs. For my case, the outputs are a joint set of high-quality [tetradisciplinary](<metapcms>) projects and intellectual contributions, combined with a general philosophy/ethic. 

## Expressivity 
An important but related goal for `RI` is its ability to act as an expressive aid. 

## Interpretive Fluency
Everyone who processes information has a "native" style by which they process it that is characteristic to the most natural operations of their mind. I want `RI` to represent my dialect and toolkit for processing and interpreting information from my environment, whatever that information is and entails. 

## Continuity and Integrity 
It's very easy to lose track of myself: what I want, what I care about, what gives my life. When I get a minute to take a breather and meditate on the question, I'm easily able to answer the question completely and thoroughly. But there are too many times where I find myself completely lost. To that end, `RI` serves as a tool for continuity control and maintenance of ontological integrity as my circumstances and experiences change around me.

# Bibliography and Inspirations
## Godel, Escher, Bach
*Douglas Hofstadter*
>*GEB* presents a model of emergence via a vivid multidisciplinary and multimodal argument, showing that formal rule-governed systems can exhibit complex, emergent, and self-referential behavior. I try to emulate his strange multidisciplinary style and intend to create a version of `RI` that displays emergent behavior like that of a society when I interact with it. 

## Man’s Search for Meaning
*Viktor Frankl*
>After multiple crises of meaning during and after college, I read Frankl’s book and realized abruptly that I needed to start crafting my own *raison d’être* if I wanted to succeed at anything I cared about.

## Dungeons and Dragons
*Critical Role*, *Dimension 20*, *One Shots*
>I obsessively binged *Critical Role* and *Dimension 20* after graduating college. I couldn't believe how a few friends sitting together at a table could create a mythology and universe so colorful, so compelling, with such awesome and gut-wrenching stories. 
>Dimension 20 provided a more comedic axis to this, but the concept the same. D&D is probably the greatest gamified, democratic storytelling medium of our time.
>Several Kernel [[Ops]] are inspired by D&D spells. The organization of `RI` is also inspired by [D&D-style worldbuilding](<Worldbuilding in D&D.md>).

## Crafting Interpreters
*Robert Nystrom*
>Nystrom’s book introduced me to programming language and compiler design. I find programming languages an incredibly useful paradigm for designing Kernel [[Ops]].

## No Bad Parts
