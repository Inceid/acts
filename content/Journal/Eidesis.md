>Ideas should be cultivated with patience and care.

# Definition
**Eidesis** is a garden of ideas; a natural world of dreams. It is the natural generator of ideas for `RI`.

It is an attempt at formalization of my informal/natural processes of dreaming, ideation, and idea selection for further refinement and implementation within `RI`. 

# Implementation 
*Eidesis* is the default initial destination of any idea that cannot be assigned an explicit/well-formed type or purpose (see [[Note Types]]).

All ideas can live in *Eidesis* forever. However, for purposes of those ideas that beg implementation, *Eidesis* implements an objective natural selection-based model of prioritization for ideas. 

## Plotting a new note 
A new note can be plotted into an *Eidesis* biome by adding a tag #eidesis to the note.

Each plot (will eventually) have a schedule for prompting the user to return to “water” the idea or further develop it. For now I manually review ideas every week or so to see if I can develop any of them further.
