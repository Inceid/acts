# Definition 
A **Praemissio** is a message from the present self to the future. 

Praemissios are best written out in analog form and stored somewhere where they will not be accessed until a designated future point in time. 

# Body 
Here’s your first praemissio. Do not forget what it was like to be in my position. In medical school. How terrible most attending physicians are as teachers. How much time was wasted sitting in lecture halls when I learned more watching short videos anyways. How much work it was, and it actually came easier to me than to many others. 

Please do not forget what it is like to not know something and to have to work to figure it out. To the extent that you continue to serve as a physician going forward, it will be good to remember what it was like being young, inexperienced, and relatively afraid, what it was like to learn to steel oneself and seek support. 

Suraj Joshi, MS3
