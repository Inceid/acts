# Primordia 
My concepts protect me against the deluge of disordered information from the world.

My stories protect me from the apparent meaninglessness of our suffering.

My conception of human nature protects me from simplistic, rash generalizations about the world and the people within it.

A thick grid of concepts protects me from falling victim to vicious cycles of untargeted defeatist emotions.

# Definition 
A **protective abstraction** is a concept that serves to interpret, rationalize or process information from the world (positive or negative) in a manner that is action-guiding or stabilizing. 

# Motivation 
Protective abstractions are a sunscreen, an armor, a set of clothes, a set of basic personal protective equipment for the world. They allow us to navigate a psychologically/existentially hostile, meaningless, and unforgiving universe without suffering irreparable damage to our integrity. 

# Examples
For most people, religion is a protective abstraction especially in cases of tragedy and trauma. 

Idealism of any kind that survives exposure to adverse experiences in the world serves as a protective abstraction. 

# Implementation
To be straightforward: protective abstractions allow me to be a kind, healthy, reliably motivated, effective, ethical, honorable, good person. 

# Deprotection 
Sometimes it’s best to take the armor off, especially with people that you’re better off being vulnerable and unprotected with.
