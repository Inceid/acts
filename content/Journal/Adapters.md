# Definition 
An **adapter** is any interface between two devices or systems. 

# Implementation 
Informally, an adapter is any note, language, or activity that translates what I’m doing in `RI`/*SS* into a format that other people can more easily understand and interpret. 

Adapters are typically implemented either as notes that serve as explanatory links between components of `RI` or as notes explaining my relationship to the world of appearances. 

`pivot: cs`
As in an operating system, a good adapter design has an adapter for every external drive/platform, or [extern](<Externs>), that interfaces with the system. 

As such, adapters are generally classified according to the extern they interface with. 

`pivot: med`
In medicine and the medical education system, the premise of rotating extensively through many of the major specialties and subspecialties is that emerging providers will likely have to interface with every medical specialty in some form. As such, they have to design an adapter model internally for interfacing with these specialties. 

`pivot: phil`
An ideological adapter is a device or interface that allows you to effectively interpret the point of view of another ideology or set of concepts in a way that *effectively emulates someone believing and practicing those concepts*.

For me, it’s desirable to have an adapter to as many large, popular, or compelling/plausible ideologies as possible. 

## Adapters in [[metapcms]]
Four kinds of adapter models are especially relevant to me: Kernel-Philosophy, Kernel-Computation, Kernel-Medicine, and Kernel-Systems. [[The Kernel]] is the core device that represents my thought patterns in structured form. An adapter is required to communicate between the kernel and each system I’m interested in understanding and working in. 

### Kernel $\leftrightarrow$ P 
Adapters from Kernel to Philosophy will consist of deliberations within the [Permanon](<Permanon>), [annotations](<Annotation>) of philosophical works, and independent essays on philosophical and political concepts of interest. 

### Kernel $\leftrightarrow$ C 
The adapters from Kernel to Computation will consist of 
- **standard** adapters and 
- **discipline-specific** adapters. 

Standard adapters are my personalized implementation of core computational models/ideas. 

Discipline-specific adapters are projects in each of the applied disciplines relevant to computer science that I’m interested in exploring. 

These are elaborated below:

#### Standard 
- [`indra`](<Designing a Programming Language>), a programming language
- [`Yantra`](<Designing an Instrument for Thought.md>), a distraction-free device for thinking and general navigation

#### Discipline-specific 
- None right now. 

### Kernel $\leftrightarrow$ M
The main adapter from Kernel to Medicine will consist of a [medical knowledgebase](<Building a Medical Knowledgebase>) housed within `RI`. 

### Kernel $\leftrightarrow$ S 
Adapters from Kernel to systems thinking will consist of annotations of foundational or introductory systems texts as well as operationalizations of systems concepts in different areas of `RI`.

## Adapters to the world
To maintain [external congruence](<External Congruence.md>), it’s necessary to have multiple robust adapters to the external world. These can be implemented as social relationships, languages and expression modalities that can be easily communicated with other minds, and other outward-facing documents or ideas.
