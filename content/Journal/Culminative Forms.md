# Definition 
A **culminative form** or **culminum** is a summative, final, highest form of a project, evoking ideas of permanence, visceral resonance, and the idea of a masterpiece or magnum opus.

A culminatice form should be memorable and immediately recognizable as a final form of a prolonged journey of effort. 

# Implementation 
Certain forms of projects have a deep, intuitive, emotional resonance for me that I’d want to see implemented as culminative forms:

1. **Sanskrit**: for any linguistically amenable project, I’d want to see a Sanskrit version for it, be it a speech, language, invented world/stoy, or other peice of writing/rhetoric. Sanskrit to me has a sufficient similarity with Hindi and an air of permanence and civilizational memory similar to Latin. 

2. **Calculus**: I’d love to be able to invent a novel calculus for higher-order, dynamic concepts that current formal languages can’t sufficiently explain. Unfortunately I don’t currently have the formal training or experience to do this. But it remains a long-term goal.
