# Definition 
A **feedback loop** is any interaction between the output of a process and the rate of the process. 

In systems theory, a feedback loop is a relationship between a [stock](Stocks.md) and an inflow/outflow of that stock. 

# Balancing Loops
A **balancing loop** reduces the inflow/outflow rate as the stock value changes. 

# Reinforcing Loops 
A **reinforcing loop** increases the inflow/outflow rate as the stock value changes. 

# Dominance
One feedback loop **dominates** another if its instantaneous effect on the stock’s quantity is larger than the other’s. 

Informally, we say a feedback loop is dominant if it is the loop that determines a system’s behavior.
