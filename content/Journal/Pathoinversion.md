# Definition
A **Pathoinversion** is the deliberate cultivation of a tendency or behavior that is socially conceived as a 'pathology' or ‘disorder’ for the purpose of 'inverting' its dysfunctional or distressing effects, instead exploiting it for one's own gain.

Pathoinversions are a specific form of **constructivistic** solutions to psychological or existential problems. These seek to actively construct solutions from one's problems to the maximal extent possible from just the concepts and situations available to oneself and one's mind.

# Depression Pathoinversions 
**Existential Depression**: the tendency to ruminate and feel depressed over broad existential threats to humanity. 

This is what we might call “congruent depression” or depression that exists for a set of concrete identifiable reasons congruent with our shared reality.
